The Founder

Trendell Fordham.

Founder, Genesis AI Systems

I am a Detroit business owner. I built Genesis AI Systems because I lived the problem firsthand. Calls slipping through the cracks every week. Voicemail piling up. Customers moving on before I even knew they had called.

Hiring a full-time receptionist was not in the budget. I tried the off-the-shelf phone answering tools. They sounded like a robot. They lost callers. They were not built for a business that runs on the phone the way mine does.

So I built one myself. Put it on my own business line first. Tuned it on real calls before it ever went out to a client. The system you would get is the same one I trust to handle my own calls every day.

Why Genesis AI Systems Exists

For local businesses that run on phone calls.

Genesis AI Systems is for the same kind of business I run. Local. Service-based. The kind of shop where the phone is how you get work, and a missed call is a job that went somewhere else.

This is not a tech company that stumbled into AI. I am not selling a platform. I am building one Riley at a time, sitting with the owner, learning how their business actually answers the phone, and shaping the call flow until it fits. Same way I would build it for myself.

One person builds it. One person tunes it. One person picks up when something needs to change. That is the whole company. By design.
