The Math of the Problem

Three reasons local businesses lose calls every week.

Missed calls equal lost revenue

A full-time receptionist in Detroit runs $4,000 to $5,000 a month once you load in benefits, taxes, and turnover. Most small contractors cannot justify that number.

Even when you can afford the hire, the new person is not useful for weeks. They learn your business slowly. They turn over before they are fully trained. Then you do it again.
