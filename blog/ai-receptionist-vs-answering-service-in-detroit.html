AI Receptionist vs. Answering Service in Detroit

September 16, 2026

A live answering service in metro Detroit typically runs $300 to $600 a month for a base block of minutes, then $1 to $2 for every minute past that. A ten-minute call to book a routine appointment can cost more than the appointment is worth, and the service still cannot put anything on your calendar. It takes a message and forwards it, which is voicemail with a person attached.

What you are actually paying for

Answering services price by the minute because their business model is staffing a call center, not running your front desk. A Dearborn auto shop that gets 40 after-hours calls a month, averaging six minutes each, burns through 240 minutes before a single busy week even happens. Once the base block runs out, every extra minute lands on the next invoice at $1 to $2 apiece. A slow month costs less, a fast month costs more, in a business that needs cost it can plan around.

What the call center cannot do

The person answering that line has never seen your schedule and does not work for your business specifically. They take a name and a number and pass it along. If a caller wants an appointment booked while they have you on the phone, that is outside what a phone-answering contractor does. Every one of those calls waits on a callback, and a Warren or Southfield customer comparing two or three shops, dental offices, or law firms in the same afternoon often books with whoever answers back first.
