CPR & Refresher

CPR cards, and a second run at the state exam.

Two smaller classes we run on request, for our own students and for anyone in Detroit who needs them.

BLS/CPR class

$65

Basic Life Support for healthcare providers: adult, child and infant CPR, the bag valve mask, choking, and the AED. Most nursing facilities and hospitals ask for this card before you start, and our CNA students usually add it to their course.

Classes are scheduled on request. Email us the date you'd like and we'll confirm a time.

State exam refresher

Call for the price and next date

For people who took a nurse aide class somewhere else, let their certification lapse, or didn't pass the exam the first time. Ms. Sam goes back over the skills the examiners check and the written material that trips people up, then you test here at the school.

Dates depend on demand, so call and tell us where you are with it.
