About

A small school at 7 Mile and Woodward.

One instructor, one classroom, and a few hundred graduates working in nursing homes and hospitals around Detroit.

Ms. Sam

Samania Peterson, RN, BSN.

Ms. Sam is a registered nurse who opened Yes We CNA in 2019 to give people in Detroit a fast, honest way into health care. She runs the school and teaches the class herself. Every certificate the school issues has her signature on it.

Her students describe the same things over and over on Google: a pace that lets everyone keep up, a room that feels like home, and an instructor who still answers the phone after you graduate. She calls her graduates family, and means it.

The mission of Yes We CNA is to provide quality education and prepare students for promising, rewarding careers in health care, and to encourage every student to pursue whatever dream they may have. We are committed to producing knowledgeable, highly skilled, caring and competent people to care for those who cannot care for themselves. From the school's mission statement

Admission

How we decide who's in.

Applications can be completed online or at the school. On the first day of class, every student has a short interview with the program coordinator. The point is to get a sense of your attitude, determination, motivation and commitment to the people you'll be caring for. There are no trick questions.

You need a valid driver's license or state ID, and a TB skin test less than two years old, which is due before you finish the program. Michigan's nurse aide registry rules on criminal history apply; call us if you have a record and we'll go through it before you pay anything.
