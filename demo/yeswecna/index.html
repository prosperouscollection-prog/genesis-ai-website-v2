How it works

From deposit to registry in two or three weeks.

  1. Reserve a seat

    Pick a date and pay the $150 deposit online, or call and pay cash at the school. Seats go in the order deposits come in.

  2. Your first day

    Bring your driver's license or state ID. You'll sit down with Ms. Sam for a short interview, pick up your burgundy scrubs, and start on the material the same day.

  3. Class, skills lab, clinical

    Mornings run 8:30 to 4 for two weeks. Evenings run 5 to 10 for three weeks. You learn on hospital beds, mannequins and blood pressure cuffs, then put in clinical hours at a nursing facility.

  4. State exam and the registry

    Take the state exam here at the school. When you pass you go on the Michigan Nurse Aide Registry, and we help you find work.

Paying for it

Four ways students cover the $750.

  • Payment plan

    $150 down. The $600 balance comes to your email as an invoice and you pay it on Fridays while you're in class. Half the balance each Friday for the morning class, $200 to $300 each Friday for the evening class.

  • Michigan Rehabilitation Services

    If you have a disability, including diabetes, high blood pressure, a mental health condition, a learning disability, or a history with alcohol or drugs, MRS may pay for tuition, scrubs, transportation and test fees. Call us and we'll walk you through the paperwork.

  • Scholarships

    We accept scholarship funding, including MiSSG and Back on Track. Bring your award letter to your first day.

  • Tuition reimbursement

    Under Section 4801 of the Omnibus Budget Reconciliation Act of 1987, a Medicaid or Medicare nursing facility that hires you within 12 months of finishing must reimburse you for the training you paid for yourself. Ask about it at your job interview.

Questions people ask on the phone

Before you put money down.

Do I need any experience?

No. Most students start with none. You need a valid driver's license or state ID and a TB test dated within the last two years. The TB test can be done during the course, but it has to be in before you finish.

Can I take the class if I have a record?

It depends on the offense and how old it is. Michigan keeps anyone with a felony conviction in the last 15 years off the nurse aide registry, along with certain abuse, neglect, theft or fraud misdemeanors in the last 10. Call us before you pay a deposit and we'll go through your situation with you.

Where do I take the state exam?

Here, in our building at 1004 W 7 Mile Rd. You don't travel to a testing center.

What do I wear?

Burgundy scrubs, every day. We sell sets for $50 at the school in men's and women's fits, XS to 3XL. Closed shoes only. No Crocs.

Is the textbook required?

No. It's $75 and it helps, especially for studying at home, but the class covers everything you need to pass.

What if I can't make a Friday payment?

Talk to Ms. Sam before Friday. Payments on the plan are due each Friday before 5 pm unless you've arranged something else. Deposits and payments are not refundable.

How soon can I work after the class?

As soon as you pass the state exam and your name is on the Michigan Nurse Aide Registry. We help graduates find placements, and many are hired by the facility where they did their clinical hours.
