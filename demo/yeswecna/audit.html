What the old site and the social accounts were doing wrong.

Audit of yeswecna.com, Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, YouTube and the Google Business Profile, taken September 11, 2026, before the redesign.

Summary

The school has more proof than almost any competitor in Detroit and the website shows none of it. Google rates it 4.7 from 150 reviews. TikTok has 5,245 followers and 45,300 likes. The state program number is printed on every certificate. The site mentions none of these. Instead, the homepage is a single 13,966 pixel scroll that opens on a silent video with no headline, runs five checkout forms in a row, lists scrubs at thirty billion dollars, and gives three different answers to "what does this cost."

The redesign is not a restyle. It fixes the facts first, then the structure, then the look.

Market context

Detroit area CNA schools checked: Williams Healthcare Training (Dearborn), Healing Healthcare Training Institute, Nursing Detroit, Serenity Health Training (Southfield), Heart to Heart (Dearborn).

  • Tuition ranges from $750 to $1,543. Yes We CNA at $750 is at the low end, but the $1,312 line makes it look mid to high.
  • Every competitor site has a pricing table, an FAQ, and a clear "Apply" or "Enroll" button in the header. Several list funding partners by name. One publishes a pass rate.
  • All of them use a blue or teal "clinical" palette. Burgundy and cream stand apart and match the scrubs students actually wear.

Accuracy notes used in the new copy

  • Felony: the 15 year rule (MCL 333.20173), not "cannot possess a felony."
  • Tuition reimbursement: Section 4801 of OBRA 1987 is the right citation. Facilities that take Medicaid or Medicare must reimburse a self-paid nurse aide hired within 12 months.
  • State exam: Michigan's vendor has been Headmaster (D&S Diversified Technologies) since July 2023; registry is the Michigan Nurse Aide Registry. The operator confirmed the school is a test site.
  • Training hours: Michigan requires the federal minimum of 75. Both schedules meet it (AM: 10 days at 7.5 hours; PM: 15 days at 5 hours).
