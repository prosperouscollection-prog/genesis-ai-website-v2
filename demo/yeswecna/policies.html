Policies

The rules, in plain words.

Read these before you pay a deposit. They apply to every student, every class.

No refunds

All sales are final. The school does not offer money-back guarantees of any kind. By paying a deposit or any part of tuition you agree that you are not entitled to a refund under any circumstances. As the school puts it: we cannot refund knowledge, so no refunds will be considered.

This covers the $150 seat deposit, Friday payments on the payment plan, payment in full, scrubs, the textbook, and the BLS/CPR class.

Deposits and seats

A seat in a specific class is held only when the $150 deposit is paid. Applications, phone calls and messages do not hold seats. Deposits are applied to tuition and are not refundable. If you need to move to a later start date, call before your class begins and we will do what we can.

Payment plan

The $600 balance is invoiced to the email address you give us and is paid while you are in class. For the morning class, half the balance is due each Friday before 5 pm. For the evening class, $200 to $300 is due each Friday before 5 pm until the balance is paid. If you need a different arrangement, discuss it with faculty ahead of time, not after a missed payment. Cash is accepted at the school. Get a receipt every time.

Dress code

Burgundy scrubs are required for class and for clinical. Sets are sold at the school for $50. Closed shoes only. No Crocs.

Admission

Applications may be completed online or at the school. Every student has an interview with the program coordinator on the first day of class, to establish attitude, determination, motivation and commitment to the people they will serve. Requirements: a valid driver's license or state ID, and a TB skin test less than two years old, which is not required to start but is due before the program ends.

Michigan's nurse aide registry rules on criminal history apply to every student. A felony conviction within the last 15 years, or certain abuse, neglect, theft or fraud misdemeanors within the last 10 years, will keep you off the registry regardless of how you do in class. If you have a record, call us before paying a deposit.

About the program

Yes We CNA, LLC is a State of Michigan proprietary nurse aide training program, NATP #N19-00006, and a state exam test site. Fees include books, laboratory supplies, training materials, lecture and clinical practicum as described on the tuition page. Under Section 4801 of the Omnibus Budget Reconciliation Act of 1987, students who pay their own tuition and are hired within 12 months by a long term care facility that accepts Medicaid or Medicare are entitled to reimbursement of their training costs from that facility.

Questions about any of this: (313) 400-7668 or info@yeswecna.com.
