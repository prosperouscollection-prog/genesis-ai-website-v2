Payment plan

How the Fridays work.

  • Day one: $150 deposit. Paid online when you reserve, or in cash at the school. Not refundable.
  • The $600 balance is invoiced to your email. You pay it during the class, not before.
  • Morning class: half the balance each Friday. $300 the first Friday, $300 the second, before 5 pm.
  • Evening class: $200 to $300 each Friday. Three Fridays, before 5 pm, until the balance is paid.
  • Something come up? Talk to Ms. Sam before Friday. Plans can be adjusted when they're discussed ahead of time, not after a missed payment.
  • Pay in full instead: $700 for either class, paid up front. Call us to set it up.

Help paying

Ways students cover the $750.

Bring paperwork from any of these to your first day, or call ahead and we'll tell you what to ask your caseworker for.

  • Michigan Rehabilitation Services

    MRS helps people with disabilities get trained and hired. That includes physical conditions like diabetes and high blood pressure, mental health conditions, learning disabilities, and a history with alcohol or drugs. MRS can cover tuition, uniforms, transportation and test fees. Call us and we'll walk you through the paperwork.

  • Scholarships

    We accept scholarship funding, including MiSSG and Back on Track. If you have an award letter from a scholarship, a church, an employer or a workforce program, bring it and we'll work with them.

  • Tuition reimbursement

    Under Section 4801 of the Omnibus Budget Reconciliation Act of 1987, a nursing facility that takes Medicaid or Medicare and hires you within 12 months of finishing your training must reimburse what you paid for it. It's the law, not a favor. Ask about it at your job interview and keep your receipts from us.

  • Cash

    We take cash at the school for the deposit and for Friday payments. Get a receipt every time.

Money questions

Asked on the phone every week.

What is the $1,312 number?

That is the school's figure for the full cost of getting certified, start to finish: tuition, books, scrubs, the state exam and the license fee. Tuition itself is $750. Call and we'll go through the breakdown with you.

Can I get my deposit back if I change my mind?

No. Deposits and payments are not refundable. If you need to move to a later start date, call us before your class begins.

Can I pay with a card?

The $150 deposit is paid online by card when you reserve your seat. Friday payments are made through the invoice sent to your email, or in cash at the school.

Does the class qualify for financial aid?

Not federal student aid like Pell grants. A two week class doesn't qualify. MRS, scholarships, workforce programs and employer reimbursement are the ways students get help.

Will a job really pay me back?

If the facility takes Medicaid or Medicare and hires you within a year of finishing, federal law requires it. Ask your employer's HR office and bring your receipt from us.
